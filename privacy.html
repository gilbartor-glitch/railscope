Privacy Policy

How RailScope collects, uses, and protects your information

RailScope, Inc. (“RailScope,” “we,” or “us”) is committed to respecting your online privacy and recognizes your need for appropriate protection and management of any personally identifiable information. This Privacy Policy applies to personal data you provide through our websites, applications, and electronic communications. By accessing or using our services you agree to this policy, and your continued use after any update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

1.The Info We Collect and Why

RailScope collects personal information to provide our products and services for network analysis and troubleshooting.

Identifying contact information

Names, email addresses, phone numbers, and social-media account names used to contact RailScope. This data is retained indefinitely unless otherwise directed, so we can confirm account ownership, inform you of developments, and provide customer support.

Account credentials

Usernames and passwords for the RailScope account system are stored for account management.

Support records

Customer-support conversations, including attachments, are saved to provide better support and inform future service and product development, and retained indefinitely unless you direct otherwise.

Alert (SMTP) data

Information used by the email-alert feature (usernames, passwords, return addresses) is stored locally on your own device and may be deleted by you at any time.

Local trace targets

IP addresses and DNS names you enter in RailScope Desktop are stored locally. They are neither accessible nor viewable by anyone other than you and may be deleted at any time.

RailScope Cloud data

When you use RailScope Cloud, trace data is stored on our cloud infrastructure provider’s servers for the duration of your Cloud subscription and can be deleted through the interface. Account credentials are stored indefinitely unless you direct otherwise.

2.How We Protect Your Information

RailScope maintains physical, electronic, and procedural safeguards reasonably necessary to ensure the protection of your personal information. All employees receive security education, and only authorized staff may access stored data, and only for explicit purposes.

You are responsible for keeping your password confidential and should not share your credentials. While we work to protect your information, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure. RailScope maintains an established protocol for managing any potential data breach; related inquiries may be directed to our Data Protection Officer at privacy@railscope.ai.

3.The Third Parties We Work With

Payment processors

Stripe and FastSpring handle payments in accordance with the PCI Data Security Standard. RailScope does not access or save any payment information, such as credit-card numbers.

Cloud infrastructure

Our cloud provider stores RailScope Cloud trace data. At no point does the provider view or access this data.

Hosting

Our hosting partner stores personal information but does not access or view any of your personal information.

External links

Our websites may contain links to external sites subject to those sites’ own privacy policies. We will never knowingly link to an external site we believe may pose a risk.

International transfer

Personal information is stored and processed on servers located within the United States. If you are not a U.S. resident, you agree to the global transfer, processing, and storage of your personal information.

4.Disclosure of Information to Third Parties

RailScope does not and will not disclose any of your personal information to third parties under any circumstance, except as described below.

  • Law enforcement. We disclose information only when subpoenaed or otherwise legally compelled, and only to the extent required by law.
  • Business partners. We may disclose personal information to our subsidiaries, affiliates, contractors, service providers, and other third parties we use to support certain business functions, such as order fulfillment, payment processing, email delivery, or marketing.
  • Business acquisition. If RailScope is acquired or its assets transferred, personal information may be included as a means to continue your service, used in the manner described in this Privacy Policy.

At no point will RailScope sell your personal information to a third party for commercial purposes.

5.Your Rights to Your Information

You have the right to access and control the personal information we hold about you. To audit, correct, modify, or delete your personal information, email privacy@railscope.ai. Complete deletion may not be possible where the information is necessary to provide a service you have paid for, or where we are required by law to keep it; in such cases we will notify you of what is retained and why.

6.Cookies and Similar Technologies

“Cookies” are short strings of information collected when you visit our site in order to enhance your web-browsing experience, for example by storing preferences such as items in a shopping cart. You can modify your web browser to prevent the collection of cookies, although doing so may impact your browsing activities. Your browser’s Help section provides instructions for disabling cookies.

7.California Privacy

California Civil Code Section 1798.83 permits California residents to request information about third-party disclosures for direct-marketing purposes. To make such a request, contact privacy@railscope.ai or write to us at the address below.

8.Children and Privacy

Our websites, apps, and communications are not intended for children under the age of 16. RailScope does not knowingly collect, store, or otherwise use any information provided by any person under the age of 16 without a parent or guardian’s consent. If a minor has submitted information, a parent or guardian should contact privacy@railscope.ai so that it may be deleted.

9.Marketing and You

RailScope may use your personal information to contact you regarding RailScope products and services, with your consent and within legal constraints. You may opt out at any time using the “unsubscribe” link at the bottom of any email, or by contacting privacy@railscope.ai.

By purchasing our products or services, you grant RailScope the right to use your company logo in marketing, sales, and other communications solely to identify your company as a RailScope customer. Likewise, you receive the right to use the RailScope logo solely to identify RailScope as a provider of services to your company. Any other use requires prior written permission. You may opt out of logo use by contacting us.

10.Changes to Our Privacy Policy

We post changes to this policy on our website, with notice provided on the home page. Material changes are communicated via email to your primary account address or by website notice. The date this Privacy Policy was last revised is identified at the top of this page. You are responsible for maintaining a current email address and for periodically checking this policy for updates.
